Quickstart · Your path to a verdict
  1. Build 75 Evidence to bring articles to the House.
  2. Win 218 House votes to impeach, then 67 Senate votes to convict and remove.
  3. Spend capital and days on moves. Every 12 seconds of active play also spends one day; 10 days make a month.
  4. Adjourn skips unused days to recover capital. Every blowback halves your capital after recovery, rounded down.
  5. Finish before the term ends. Keep support at 12% or higher at each monthly review. Pause or hide the tab to stop the clock.
  6. Every move can fail. Cards show success odds and rewards; stronger public support improves your odds.
  7. Public support shifts after every move and active-play day. Paused time does not affect it.

How scoring works

Victory: 10,000. Evidence: 10 each (up to 75). House votes: 10 each (up to 218). Senate votes: 30 each (up to 67). Support: 10 each. Capital: 1 each. Wins also earn 5 per remaining session day.
